El Nino set to intensify before peaking in December, UN warns

By France 24 • 2026-10-08T06:09:13+00:00

The United Nations warned on Thursday that El Nino will intensify before peaking in December, bringing higher temperatures to much of the planet and compounding the effects of man-made climate change. The World Meteorological Organization added that this naturally occurring weather phenomenon could be the strongest since at least 1950.
