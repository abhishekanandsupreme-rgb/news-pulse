OpenAI Reveals Exactly How ChatGPT Predicts Your Age

By NDTV Gadgets • 2026-10-07T10:34:54+00:00

OpenAI introduced age prediction in ChatGPT earlier this year. The system helps the AI chatbot determine whether an account may belong to someone under the age of 18. The San Francisco-based AI company has now detailed how its age prediction functionality works. As per OpenAI, it adds an extra layer of protection beyond the date of birth provided when an account is created. Instead of only relying on a user’s stated age, it looks at behavioural and account-level signals.
