Tiny photosynthetic organisms help transport carbon to the depths of the Baltic Sea

By Phys.org • 2026-10-07T23:40:01+00:00

Tiny photosynthetic organisms in the Baltic Sea can contribute substantially to the transport of carbon from surface waters to deeper parts of the sea. A new study from the Department of Ecology, Environment and Plant Sciences at Stockholm University shows that their ability to form colonies and aggregates is an important factor determining how much of their biomass sinks.
