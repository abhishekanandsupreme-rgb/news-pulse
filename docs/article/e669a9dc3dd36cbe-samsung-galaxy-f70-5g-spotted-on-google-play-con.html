Samsung Galaxy F70 5G Spotted on Google Play Console With Galaxy A17 5G Codename, BIS Database

By NDTV Gadgets • 2026-10-07T11:33:16+00:00

Samsung Galaxy F70 5G has appeared on Google's Supported Devices List with the model number SM-E176E and codename a17x. The same codename is associated with the Galaxy A17 5G, suggesting that Samsung could introduce the F70 5G as a rebranded version of the A-series phone. The handset has also reportedly received BIS certification in India. Its specifications have not been revealed, although it could share some hardware with the Galaxy A17 5G. Samsung has yet to announce its launch details.
