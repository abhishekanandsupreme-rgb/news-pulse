Priscilla Chan founded a school; 10 years later, Board votes to close it

By Times of India • 2026-10-07T09:00:48+00:00

Priscilla Chan's vision of a tuition-free educational environment for low-income families faced closure after a decade of significant funding. As the board made the difficult decision to shut down due to certain funding withdrawal, students found themselves with mixed academic results, some even excelling in literacy. With 540 students affected, the Chan Zuckerberg Initiative has stepped in to provide financial aid to families and local organizations in the aftermath.
