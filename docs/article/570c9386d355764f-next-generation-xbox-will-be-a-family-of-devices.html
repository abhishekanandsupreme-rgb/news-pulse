Next-Generation Xbox Will Be a 'Family of Devices', Says Xbox CEO Asha Sharma

By NDTV Gadgets • 2026-10-07T14:04:56+00:00

Xbox CEO Asha Sharma reportedly told staff at a town hall meeting on Tuesday that Project Helix, the next-generation Xbox, will be a “family of devices”. The executive said that the tenth generation of Xbox consoles will also cater to people “on the go”, suggesting the console family will include gaming handhelds.
