Meerut: Woman, lover murder husband, stage snakebite death with live cobra

By Times of India • 2026-10-07T16:32:15+00:00

A Meerut court convicted Ravita Kashyap and Amar Deep for the murder of Amit Kashyap. The court found that Amit had died of asphyxia rather than a snakebite, contradicting initial claims. The couple allegedly planned the murder, using a live snake to create the illusion of a snakebite. Evidence included testimony from a snake charmer who provided the snake. Sentencing for the convicted individuals is scheduled for October 16, 2025.
