After $6.25B 'gift' to Trump Accounts, Michael Dell's wife makes an 'appeal' to parents

By Times of India • 2026-10-08T08:08:52+00:00

Michael and Susan Dell urged parents to claim funds in newly established 'Trump Accounts' for children. These accounts, created under a new federal tax law, aim to provide children with financial advantages. Eligible American children can receive a one-time contribution to encourage future investments. The Dells have committed over $6 billion to support these accounts for the next generation.
