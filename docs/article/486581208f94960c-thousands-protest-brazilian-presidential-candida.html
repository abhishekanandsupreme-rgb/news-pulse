Thousands protest Brazilian presidential candidate Flavio Bolsonaro, fearing far-right return

By France 24 • 2026-10-07T22:01:02+00:00

Thousands of people joined a student march in downtown Rio de Janeiro on Wednesday to protest against presidential candidate Flavio Bolsonaro ahead of Brazil's October 25 presidential runoff. Bolsonaro led Sunday's first-round vote, prompting demonstrators to rally behind incumbent President Luiz Inacio Lula da Silva and oppose the far right's return to power.
