Snowball Earth's iron deposits may have formed through microbes living without sunlight

By Phys.org • 2026-10-08T00:00:07+00:00

Iron is one of the most abundant metals on Earth's surface, yet how such massive iron ore deposits formed throughout geological history has long remained a mystery. The prevailing theory has been that ancient photosynthetic bacteria oxidized iron, causing it to precipitate and accumulate in the oceans.
