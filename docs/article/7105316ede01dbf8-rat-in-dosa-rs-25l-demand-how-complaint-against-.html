Rat in dosa, Rs 25L demand: How complaint against Mumbai hotel took an extortion turn

By Times of India • 2026-10-08T07:06:36+00:00

A rat was allegedly found in a masala dosa at a Borivli hotel by two sisters. They reportedly demanded Rs 25 lakh as compensation after the incident. The hotelier paid them Rs 1 lakh but later received continued demands from them. As a result, the hotelier approached the police for assistance. An FIR has since been registered against the sisters for extortion.
