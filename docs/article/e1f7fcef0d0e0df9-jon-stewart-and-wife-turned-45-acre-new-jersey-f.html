Jon Stewart and wife turned 45-acre New Jersey farm into animal sanctuary

By Times of India • 2026-10-08T08:30:00+00:00

In 2016, Jon Stewart and Tracey Stewart acquired Hockhockson Farm in Colts Neck, New Jersey, with the vision of establishing both a working farm and a sanctuary for rescued farm animals. Their ambitious plans include educational programs focused on sustainable farming and healthy eating. The property is equipped with barns and pastures, as well as facilities dedicated to animal care, successfully fulfilling its mission of promoting agriculture and welfare.
