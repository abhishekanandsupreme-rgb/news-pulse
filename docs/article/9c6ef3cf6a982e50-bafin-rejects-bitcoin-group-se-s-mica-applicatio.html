BaFin Rejects Bitcoin Group SE’s MiCA Application for Futurum Bank After 15-Month Review

By NDTV Gadgets • 2026-10-07T11:45:54+00:00

Bitcoin Group’s Futurum Bank is exploring partnerships with licensed German firms after BaFin rejected its MiCA licence application. The bank had planned to operate Bitcoin.de’s revamped crypto exchange itself, but will now consider using external firms for trading and custody. Bitcoin Group can object to the decision within one month of receiving the formal notification.
