Oppo Find X10 Series Hands-on Image Surfaces Online Ahead of India Launch

By NDTV Gadgets • 2026-10-07T13:43:26+00:00

Oppo Find X10 series will launch in India soon. Ahead of it, an alleged live image of the Find X10 series model has leaked online. The leaked image shows the handset running ColorOS 17. The software is based on Android 17 and is also seen on the Find X10 models launched in China. Oppo launched the Find X10, Find X10 E, and Find X10 Pro Max in China last month with a starting price of CNY 5,499 (roughly Rs. 78,000).
