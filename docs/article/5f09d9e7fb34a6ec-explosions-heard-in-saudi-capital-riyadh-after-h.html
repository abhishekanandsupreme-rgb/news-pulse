Explosions heard in Saudi capital Riyadh after Houthis' deadly attacks on airports

By France 24 • 2026-10-08T03:02:35+00:00

Several explosions were reported on Thursday in Saudi Arabia’s capital Riyadh amid ongoing fighting between the kingdom and Yemen's Houthis.
