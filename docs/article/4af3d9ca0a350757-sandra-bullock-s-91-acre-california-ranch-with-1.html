Sandra Bullock’s 91-acre California ranch with 1,200 avocado trees sells for $5.6M

By Times of India • 2026-10-07T22:20:00+00:00

In 2007, Sandra Bullock acquired a sprawling 91-acre ranch in Valley Center for $2.725 million. After transforming it with extensive renovations and establishing an organic avocado farm, she sold the property for $5.6 million in 2024. Encompassing a beautifully refurbished adobe house, a guest cottage, and facilities for agricultural operations, the ranch stands as a perfect blend of luxury and sustainability for its future owner.
