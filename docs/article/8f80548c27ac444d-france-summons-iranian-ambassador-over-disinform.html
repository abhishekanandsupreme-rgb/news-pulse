France summons Iranian ambassador over 'disinformation' on student protests

By France 24 • 2026-10-07T17:23:52+00:00

The French foreign ministry on Wednesday summoned the Iranian ambassador to protest against a "disinformation campaign conducted by the Iranian authorities" over the student protests that have rocked France. The move came a day after Tehran summoned the French ambassador over the treatment of protesters in France.
