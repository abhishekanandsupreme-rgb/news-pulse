Sony ULT Tower 5, Tower 7 and Tower Max With Up to 2,150W Output Launched in India: Price, Specifications

By NDTV Gadgets • 2026-10-07T10:02:06+00:00

Sony has launched three new party speakers in India under its second-generation ULT Power Sound range. The ULT Tower 5 and ULT Tower 7 come with built-in batteries and are rated to deliver up to 20 and 30 hours of playback, respectively. The ULT Tower Max is the flagship model with a 2,150W output and three 280mm woofers. All three models offer party-focused features, including karaoke and DJ connectivity, while the Tower Max also gets a 100-LED lighting system.
