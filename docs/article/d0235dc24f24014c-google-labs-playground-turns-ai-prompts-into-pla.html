Google Labs Playground Turns AI Prompts Into Playable Games Without Any Coding

By NDTV Gadgets • 2026-10-08T04:26:29+00:00

Google on Wednesday announced Playground as an experimental gaming platform. As per the Mountain View-based tech giant, it lets users create, play, and share custom browser-based games using natural-language prompts. They can start with a blank canvas or remix prompts, then describe changes to gameplay, characters, physics, and environments through a conversational interface. Apart from this, Google is preparing a deeper integration with Unity Spark, adding further capabilities to Playground.
