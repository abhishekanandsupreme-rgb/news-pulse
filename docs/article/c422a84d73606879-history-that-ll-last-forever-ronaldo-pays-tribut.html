‘History that'll last forever’: Ronaldo pays tribute to Messi with ‘utmost respect’

By Times of India • 2026-10-08T07:48:36+00:00

Lionel Messi concluded his international career with a farewell match where he scored and assisted in a victory. Cristiano Ronaldo, his rival, honored Messi's achievements and expressed his respect in a social media post. More than 85,000 fans attended the match at Buenos Aires’ Monumental Stadium, celebrating Messi's legacy. Messi reflected on his 21-year journey representing Argentina and the emotions tied to his decision.
